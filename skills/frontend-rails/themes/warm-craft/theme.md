# warm-craft

Handmade and friendly. A soft serif for headings (Young Serif), rounded sans body (Nunito Sans), cream paper, terracotta accent, softly rounded corners.

**Good for:** cafés, bakeries, shops, makers, local services, food and drink.
**Not great for:** developer tools, finance, dense data.

**Make it work:**
- Use real photos of the product or place. A plain `--surface-2` block is fine as a placeholder.
- Write like a person: short sentences, specific details (days, places, prices).
- Use the accent for the main action and small details like step numbers.
- Sections can sit on `--surface-2` panels to break up the page.

**Feels like:** a good independent coffee roaster's website.
