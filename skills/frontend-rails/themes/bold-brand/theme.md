# bold-brand

Loud and confident. Archivo Black headlines at very large sizes, Work Sans body, cream or near-black background, hot orange accent, hard offset shadows.

**Good for:** launches, campaigns, events, consumer brands with attitude.
**Not great for:** dashboards, long reports, anything that needs to feel quiet.

**Make it work:**
- One huge headline per screen. Short copy.
- Big color blocks: a full-width `--accent` band with `--accent-contrast` text works well.
- In light mode, use `--link` (dark orange) for links; the bright `--accent` is for fills, not text.
- Hard shadows (`--shadow-1`, `--shadow-2`) on buttons and cards, no soft shadows.

**Feels like:** a festival or a bold product launch page.
