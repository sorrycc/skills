# soft-consumer

Friendly and calm. Bricolage Grotesque headings, Figtree body, warm off-white, coral accent, sage-green success, generous rounded corners.

**Good for:** consumer apps, wellness, booking, onboarding flows, personal finance for normal people.
**Not great for:** developer tools, serious reports.

**Make it work:**
- Use big rounded panels (`.panel`) to group things.
- Plenty of space; one clear action per screen.
- Friendly, plain copy. Use the user's words.
- Illustrations or photos of people help; abstract blobs don't.

**Feels like:** a modern habit tracker or booking app.
